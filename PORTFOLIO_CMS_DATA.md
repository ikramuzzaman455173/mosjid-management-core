## 🎯 Problem & Direct Impact

- **Before:** Mosque collections, recurring fees, and expenses were kept across paper notebooks, while volunteers used handheld remotes to manually adjust prayer clocks in the prayer hall.
- **Engineered Solution:** Unified multi-fund ledgers, 1-click printable PDF receipts, biometric member ID cards with QR verification, and an automated 24/7 widescreen Smart TV prayer countdown screen.
- **Measurable Result:** Monthly financial audit time reduced from 2 weeks to **instant real-time aggregation (99% faster)** with zero manual clock adjustments.
